## Deadline

- **Submission opens:** Saturday, November 14, 2026, 09:00 local time of the team's Local Event.
- **Submission closes:** **Sunday, November 15, 2026, 23:59 local time** of the team's Local Event
  (Universal Event: the Team Owner's local time).
- That is about **39 hours**, not 48. Local judging times may differ; check with the Local Lead.
- Teams may not start working on the challenge before the hackathon.

## Eligibility for global judging

- [ ] Respond to an official challenge (ours: Harmonization of MODIS and VIIRS Hot Spots).
- [ ] All members registered, same Local Event (not waitlisted), listed on the team's Members tab. Max 6 members.
      No new members after submission closes.
- [ ] All required fields on the Project page completed.
- [ ] A demo: **video ≤ 30 seconds** or **slide deck ≤ 7 slides** (including title and intro). Exceeding the limit = not eligible.
- [ ] **English** on the project page and in **all linked materials** (repo included). A video may be in another
      language only with English subtitles. Slides must be in English.
- [ ] Use **at least one NASA data source or resource** (FIRMS).
- [ ] Submit via the team page → Project tab → Edit → Submit, before the deadline.
- [ ] Tick both confirmation boxes (terms; original work, all external resources listed in References, no
      unlicensed copyrighted material).

## AI ("Super Intelligence") rules

- AI tools are allowed to accelerate development.
- AI **must not be used to come up with the idea** of how to solve the challenge.
- We must clearly indicate where and how AI was used:
  - **Code and data:** acknowledged in descriptive text and metadata.
  - **Images and video:** visible watermark.
  - **Audio:** acknowledged in text and metadata.
- AI-generated content must not use or modify NASA branding (logos, flags, mission identifiers).
- Judges see the AI indicators in the project and weigh originality, intent and execution.

Track AI use as it happens in [../resources.md](../resources.md#ai-usage).

## Judging criteria (each scored 1–5)

| Criterion | What judges look for |
|-----------|----------------------|
| **Impact** | Size of the problem addressed and how many people it helps |
| **Creativity** | Originality; a new approach or a meaningful improvement on existing ones |
| **Validity** | Scientifically sound, technically feasible, works for its purpose, usable |
| **Relevance** | Fit to the challenge |
| **Presentation** | Clarity of the submission and demo |

## Demo tips (from the guide)

- Video (≤ 30 s): who you are and the team → the challenge → project name and tagline → how it works, showing the
  prototype and how NASA data makes it possible → future impact.
- Slides (≤ 7): team name, member names, chosen challenge, project image, user experience, how NASA data enables it.
- Assign one person to lead the demo early.
