## 23:15 UTC · DECISION · Kali

Calibration specifics: universe, January 2012, pooling region, choice of K and M

What: (1) Zeros are filled only for L2 cells with at least one Aqua or S-NPP detection in the calibration years; other cells get no background estimate. (2) January 2012 is excluded from calibration. (3) The pooling region is the whole Northeast extent per month of year and day/night. (4) K and M are chosen separately by lowest leave-one-year-out MAE at cell-month level, ties to more pooling. Why: (1) avoids inventing hotspots over ocean and never-burning land; (2) S-NPP starts on 2012-01-20, a partial month; (3) matches the worked example and is the simplest form to validate first; (4) K only acts on months with Aqua > 0 and M on months with Aqua = 0. Alternatives: every cell of the bounding box as universe (adds phantom background); keeping January 2012 (biases the ratio low); pooling to the parent 1-degree cell first (kept as a candidate for validation in H10, since the extent mixes Cerrado and Caatinga); one joint K = M (less flexible). Spec: docs/spec/harmonization.md section 6.
