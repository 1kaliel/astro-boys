## 00:46 UTC · DECISION · Kali

Pooling weights chosen by Poisson deviance; pooling to the 1-degree parent cell added

What: K and M are now chosen by leave-one-year-out mean Poisson deviance instead of absolute error; the weight grid is widened to 0, 2, 5, 10, 20, 50, 100, 200; pooling to the L1 parent cell (itself pooled to the extent) is a candidate chosen together with the weights. Why: on 2012-2019 alone, absolute error picked M = 0 and K at the edge of the grid. For counts that are zero in most months, absolute error is minimized by predicting zero, so it removes the background and biases totals low; deviance scores the predicted mean. The parent level beats whole-extent pooling on every leave-one-year-out score. Result on the Northeast: pool = l1, K = 50, M = 5. Alternatives: keep absolute error (biased choice of M); squared error (dominated by a few large cells); whole-extent pooling only. Note: this change was made after a first look at the 2020-2021 test table, but was decided on calibration-year evidence; both runs are recorded in docs/methodology.md. The pass/fail rule was not changed.

## 01:16 UTC · DECISION · Kali

Observation gaps are detected and never read as zero

What: astro-fire coverage builds daily region-wide counts per satellite and flags a day as missing when the satellite recorded at most 10 percent of what the others imply, at least 30 were expected, for three or more days in a row; a month is complete with at least 90 percent of its days observed. Months incomplete for Aqua or S-NPP are left out of calibration and of the main validation scores. Why: FIRMS lists detections only, so an outage looks like a quiet period; August 2020 (Aqua) was the largest miss of every method. Found 15 gaps in 2003-2025, 11 incomplete months. Alternatives: zero-detection days only (misses degraded days: Aqua recorded a few detections on most days of its August 2020 outage); any single low day (flags 280 ordinary days, since MODIS swaths leave strips uncovered at low latitudes); flagging months by an unusual sensor ratio (selects on the quantity being modeled). Dates still to be checked against instrument notices.

## 01:16 UTC · DECISION · Kali

Background is Terra-aware: Terra is a witness, not an added count

What: zero-Aqua months are split by whether Terra recorded anything in the same cell, month and day/night period, with one pooled background for each case; the plain background applies when Terra is unknown. Why: on 2012-2019, where Aqua recorded nothing S-NPP averaged 7.2 detections when Terra saw something and 0.8 when it did not; leave-one-year-out deviance on those months falls from 2.56 to 2.19. ## 01:38 UTC · DECISION · Kali

Cell estimates are reconciled with the regional ratio (lambda = 0.75)

What: after the cell estimates are computed, each month and day/night period is scaled by (1 - lambda) + lambda x (regional Aqua x ratio for that month and period) / sum of cell estimates; not applied when the region has fewer than 30 Aqua detections. lambda = 0.75. Why: the cell model was better per cell but behind the simple ratio on regional totals. On 2012-2019, out-of-year cell-month deviance falls from 3.616 to 3.566 (1.4 percent; the rule set before running was lowest deviance, and at least 0.5 percent gain to add the step) and the regional monthly MAE from 2,963 to 1,853. On the development years the regional MAE goes from 2,612 to 2,283 against 2,371 for the best baseline, and cell MAE from 3.11 to 3.07. Alternatives: lambda = 1 (regional total fixed to the ratio; slightly worse deviance); no reconciliation; separate lambdas by season or day/night (more parameters, not tried). Consequence: estimates must be computed for the whole extent at once. Kali's idea.

## 02:03 UTC · DECISION · Cleber

D2 area sum: cells that overlap the box; absent rows count as zero only in months with coverage

What: an area is the 0.25 degree cells that overlap the box (edges that only touch are out); a box smaller than a cell selects that cell. The calendar sums count_harmonized over those cells first, then computes everything else on the sum. A month with no row anywhere in the extent is absent from the answer ('No data'); a month with rows elsewhere counts as 0 detections for area cells without a row. Why: a first version took cells whose center was inside the box, and the check against a direct Parquet sum of calendar/v1 showed it dropped 73 edge cells of the pilot clip (Aug 2010: 132.6 k against 133.8 k); with overlap the same query matches the direct sums (133.8 k, 289.9 k, 7.7 k, 120.4 k) and the 3 cells left out sit on the clip's north edge (41 of 12.07 million detections). The data contract says an absent row means no detection by the source satellite, not a confirmed absence of fire, so zero is only claimed when the month was observed somewhere. Alternatives: center inside the box (loses edge cells, errors on small boxes); any touch including edges (double counts neighbours); treating every absent month as zero (turns coverage gaps into 'quiet').

## 02:03 UTC · DECISION · Cleber

D2 median and anomalies: computed in the backend on the area sum; pending intervals stay null

What: median_hist, anomaly_abs, anomaly_pct and the robust z (1.4826 x MAD) are computed per month of the year on the area sum, over the complete (not partial) months of the baseline years (default: all years in the data; baseline_start and baseline_end can narrow it). Fewer than 3 baseline years: no median. A partial month gets the median but no anomaly. Percent is null when the median is 0, z is null when the MAD is 0. ci_low and ci_high stay null for estimated months until the pipeline delivers them (H11; today 100 percent of the 1,016,322 estimated rows have null intervals and variance); the UI says 'pending'. When the variance arrives the interval of a sum is count +/- 1.645 x sqrt(sum of variances) (variances add, as the data contract says). Why: the contract says sum cells first and never average per-cell anomalies; the real calendar/v1 has null median and anomaly columns until H11; a partial month compared with full months would read as little activity. Alternatives: precomputed per-cell anomalies (the contract forbids averaging them); filling pending intervals with zero or with the count (false precision).
